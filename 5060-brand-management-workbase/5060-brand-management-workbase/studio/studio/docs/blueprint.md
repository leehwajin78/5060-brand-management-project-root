# **App Name**: PrimeBrand AI

## Core Features:

- Landing Page Display: Render the complete landing page, including the prominent hero section with main title, subtitle, and primary CTA, the value proposition section with three key points and icons, and the footer with an additional CTA.
- Brand Diagnosis Form: Provide a structured form for users to input detailed information relevant to their professional background and branding needs.
- AI Brand Analysis Tool: Utilize an AI model to process submitted user data from the diagnosis form and generate a precise, tailored brand diagnostic report.
- Personalized Report Display: Present the generated AI brand diagnosis report to the user in a clear, actionable, and professionally formatted digital report.

## Style Guidelines:

- Light color scheme, projecting professionalism and clarity.
- Primary color: Deep indigo (#2231AC), chosen for its association with expertise, trustworthiness, and sophistication, contrasting well with a light background.
- Background color: Subtle cool gray (#EEEFF6), a heavily desaturated variant of the primary hue, providing a clean and understated canvas.
- Accent color: Vibrant sky blue (#45BBED), analogous to the primary color, offering a fresh visual pop for calls to action and highlighting key information, ensuring strong contrast.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif) for its modern, scientific, and bold feel, perfect for titles like '나다운 브랜딩 — 5060 프리미엄 브랜드 진단'.
- Body font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for excellent readability, a neutral aesthetic, and its suitability for displaying report details and explanatory text.
- Clean, contemporary line-art or minimalist filled icons for the 'Value Proposition' section, conveying clarity and professionalism.
- A clear, hierarchical layout with generous white space to enhance readability and a premium feel. CTA buttons should be prominent and easily identifiable.
- Subtle and sophisticated hover effects on interactive elements like buttons, and smooth transitions for form submissions and report generation feedback, indicating progress without distraction.
